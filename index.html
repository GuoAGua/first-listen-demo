These are past popularity results. They cannot tell us which new songs will become hits.

How does this demo work?

The model uses 12 audio features, including musical key. The cards show only four easy features. Artist names identify songs, but the model does not use them.

It ranks 5,557 test songs and sends the first 556 for review. A song counts as popular when its past popularity score is 61 or higher.

These five examples show both useful choices and mistakes. They do not measure the model’s full performance. The model score sets the order; it is not a chance of future success.
